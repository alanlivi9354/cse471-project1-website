How it works

The basic effects component takes one mixed stereo frame and copies the left and right samples to the output. It passes the audio through unchanged, which is the 10-point requirement. The sequencer calls it for every frame, then applies the score's master gain.

I used the organ demo for this component. It has only organ notes, and the audio goes through the same effects component used for the song.
