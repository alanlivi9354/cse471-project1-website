Components

I am the owner of all four components. Organ is my assigned instrument. I used the solo-project requirement of basic effects through 10/50 and organ through 40/50. Additive and wavetable are my two extra instruments.

  • Organ — required instrument, through 40/50.
  • Basic effects — required effects, 10/50.
  • Additive — extra instrument 1, through 50/50.
  • Wavetable — extra instrument 2, through 50/50.

Each page explains the component and includes its demo and score. The effects page uses the organ demo.

Score format

I used the Step 5 XML format with the .score extension. The score element sets bpm, beatspermeasure, output duration in seconds, and master gain. Each instrument element names Organ, Additive, or Wavetable and contains its notes.

Each note gives its measure, beat, duration in beats, and pitch in note, such as C4. Measures and beats start at 1. volume sets the note level, and pan runs from -1 (left) to 1 (right). Envelope times are in seconds; sustain is a level from 0 to 1. The component pages explain their extra fields.

Step 5 framework

I started with the course Step 5 Synthie framework. The sequencer mixes and writes one stereo frame at a time. Additive and wavetable share the ADSR envelope and XML helpers. Organ has its own attack and release. The waveforms are made with math; there are no outside audio samples.
